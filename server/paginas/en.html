How conText works

This video shows conText from start to finish: how to upload your material, how to talk with the tutor and how to read the graph of ideas that builds up as you speak.

The video is in Spanish; conText itself works in English and Spanish.

  1. Upload your material

    A photo of your notebook, a PDF or a text. conText reads it and tells you what it understood.

  2. Talk with the tutor

    A Socratic tutor: it asks you questions instead of answering for you. It doesn't grade you and doesn't write for you.

  3. Watch your ideas connect

    Every idea you name appears on the graph and links to the others.

  4. Download and continue later

    Button 1 · Graph XML saves your conversation and graph; upload it another day and pick up where you left off. If you don't delete the session, it deletes itself in one hour.
